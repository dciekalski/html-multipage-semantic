About NUMA Code Coffee

Ever felt the need to desperately code and drink one gallon of cappuccino in the morning? You came to the right place!

Our Story

NUMA Code Coffee started with a simple idea: a place where good coffee and focused work go hand in hand. We were tired of cafés where the Wi-Fi drops every ten minutes and there's never a free socket in sight. So we built the café we always wanted to work in ourselves.

Our Coffee

Every cup starts with carefully selected beans from small roasteries. Our baristas prepare everything by hand – from a classic espresso to an oat milk flat white for your next debugging session. And if you need something without caffeine: we've got fresh tea, homemade lemonade and snacks for long days, too.

A Place to Work and Meet

Fast Wi-Fi, plenty of sockets and quiet corners make NUMA the perfect spot to work, study or finally finish that side project. Once a month, we host small meetups where developers, students and creative minds come together to share ideas – over coffee, of course.
